NBA PROP COMMAND CENTER — v6 — IPHONE INSTRUCTIONS
================================================================
This is a separate, beginner-friendly guide just for using the app on an
iPhone. For the full list of what changed in v6, Windows setup, and test
results, see README.txt in this same folder — this file only covers the
iPhone-specific steps.


1. OPENING THE APP ON YOUR IPHONE
================================================================
The simplest way: someone sends you a link to where this app is hosted
(for example, a web address your family/you set up), and you open that
link in Safari on your iPhone. This app does not come with its own
hosting — if nobody has put it on a website yet, ask whoever set up your
Windows copy how they'd like to share a link with you, or see README.txt
section 9 for how a local server can be set up (this requires both
devices on the same home Wi-Fi).

Once you have a working link:
1. Open Safari (this MUST be Safari for the Home Screen step below to
   work — Chrome or other browsers on iPhone can't add apps to your Home
   Screen the same way).
2. Go to the link.
3. The app loads and looks the same as on a computer, just sized for your
   phone's screen.


2. ADDING IT TO YOUR HOME SCREEN (OPTIONAL BUT RECOMMENDED)
================================================================
This makes the app open like a regular app — full screen, with its own
icon — instead of living inside a Safari tab.

1. With the app open in Safari, tap the Share icon (the square with an
   arrow pointing up, usually at the bottom of the screen).
2. Scroll down in the share menu and tap "Add to Home Screen."
3. You can rename it if you'd like (e.g. "NBA Props") — then tap "Add"
   in the top-right corner.
4. An icon now appears on your Home Screen like any other app. Tap it
   any time to open the app directly, full-screen.

IMPORTANT ABOUT THIS ICON: it's a shortcut to the same web app, not a
separate app with its own copy of anything. Opening it still uses
Safari's own storage underneath — see section 3 below for what that
actually means for your data.


3. YOUR DATA ON THIS IPHONE — PLEASE READ THIS PART
================================================================
This app saves everything (your bets, parlays, ladders) directly on this
iPhone, inside Safari's local storage. There is no account and nothing
is uploaded anywhere by this app.

WHAT THAT MEANS, IN PLAIN TERMS:
  - Your iPhone's data and your Windows PC's data are TWO COMPLETELY
    SEPARATE COPIES. Adding a bet on your PC does NOT make it show up on
    your iPhone, and vice versa — not automatically, not ever, unless
    you manually move a backup file between them (see section 4).
  - Your data on this iPhone WILL survive: closing the app, restarting
    your phone, not opening it for weeks, a normal iOS update.
  - Your data on this iPhone will NOT survive: deleting the Home Screen
    icon and also clearing Safari's website data for this page,
    "Clear History and Website Data" in Safari settings, restoring your
    iPhone from scratch, or setting up a new iPhone (unless you restore
    a full device backup that includes this).
  - If you ever see "Low on storage" from iOS generally (not from this
    app specifically), that's an iPhone-wide situation, not something
    this app caused.

If this app will be your main copy of your bet tracking, the safest habit
is: export a backup file every so often (next section) so you have a copy
that doesn't depend on this one phone.


4. SAVING A BACKUP FILE FROM YOUR IPHONE
================================================================
A backup is a single file containing everything — bets, parlays, ladders,
history. You can make one any time, and you should make one before doing
anything you're unsure about (like a big cleanup in Bet History).

1. Open the app.
2. Tap the "Export Backup" button (near the top, or inside the new
   Settings tab under "Data Management & Backups").
3. Your iPhone's normal Share Sheet appears (the same share menu you'd
   see sharing a photo).
4. Tap "Save to Files."
5. Choose where to save it — "On My iPhone" is simplest, or pick iCloud
   Drive if you want it to also show up on your other Apple devices
   automatically through iCloud (that's Apple's own iCloud syncing, not
   anything this app does).
6. Tap "Save." The file is now saved with today's date in its name, e.g.
   NBA-Prop-Command-Center-Backup-2026-10-22.json — you'll find it later
   in the Files app, in whichever folder you picked.

That's it — that file is now a safe, separate copy of everything, sitting
in the Files app, independent of whether anything happens to the app
itself.


5. RESTORING A BACKUP FILE ON YOUR IPHONE
================================================================
Use this if you want to bring data from a backup file INTO this iPhone —
for example, a backup you exported from your Windows PC, or an older
backup of this same phone.

1. Open the app.
2. Tap "Import Backup" (same two places as Export — top of the app, or
   inside Settings → Data Management & Backups).
3. The Files app opens automatically so you can browse to your backup
   file (look in whichever folder you saved it to — "On My iPhone,"
   iCloud Drive, etc.).
4. Tap the backup file to select it.
5. The app checks the whole file first. If it doesn't look like a real
   backup from this app, you'll see a clear message explaining that, and
   nothing on your phone changes.
6. If the file looks good, you're asked to choose:
     MERGE (recommended for most people) — adds anything from the backup
     that isn't already on this iPhone, and leaves everything already
     here exactly as it was. Perfectly safe to do this more than once
     with the same file — nothing gets duplicated.
     REPLACE ALL DATA — erases everything currently on this iPhone and
     loads only what's in that backup file instead. There's an extra
     confirmation step for this one because it can't be undone — make
     sure you really want to start over from that file before confirming.
7. After either choice, you'll see a short summary of exactly what was
   added or changed.


6. USING IT OFFLINE ON YOUR IPHONE
================================================================
If you've added it to your Home Screen (section 2) and opened it at least
once while online, you can keep using it with no signal or Wi-Fi at all:
you can still view every saved bet, add a new one, edit a ladder, and
everything you do is still saved normally on your phone. A banner at the
top will let you know you're offline, and live scores simply won't update
until your connection comes back — the app always shows you when the
numbers were last actually refreshed rather than pretending old numbers
are current. The moment you're back online, it catches up automatically.

If you never added it to your Home Screen and are just using it in a
regular Safari tab, it generally still needs an initial connection to
load the page itself the first time each day — the Home Screen version
is the more reliable one for true offline use.


7. QUICK TROUBLESHOOTING
================================================================
- "My bets disappeared!" — First check you're opening the exact same
  Home Screen icon / the exact same link as before; a different link or
  a different browser (Safari vs. Chrome) has its own separate storage
  and will look empty even though your real data is untouched elsewhere.
  If you do have a backup file (section 4), Import it (section 5) to
  bring everything back.
- "It looks like it's stuck loading / outdated" — pull down on the page
  to refresh, or close and reopen it from the Home Screen icon.
- "I want this on a new iPhone" — export a backup on the old phone
  (section 4), get that file onto the new phone (AirDrop, email it to
  yourself, or iCloud Drive if you used that), open the app fresh on the
  new phone, then Import that backup file (section 5).
